Painting Circle -- used for research. Crafted with Pebble x12, Leaf cord x6, shows 6 stones in a circle, have intermediate sprites for amount of paintings made that shows scribbles on them. Must keep the paintings as a persistent resource to "store" the research. 12 hours for each painting, persists on the Painting Circle so you don't have to finish it all at once. No bills, tooltip shows X/6 progress.
