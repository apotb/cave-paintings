Summary of WIP techs generated by Cursor:

Traps -- cord snares. An animal that walks over one is caught or downed. A trap job resets and baits them.

Spike Traps -- the hafted, pointy version of snares.

Pit Traps -- the dug version. Same idea, bigger install.

Tree Sowing -- plant trees. A job action.

Husbandry -- parent tech. Hide the job until Herding. Settlers have nothing to do on it until then.

Herding -- fences, a gate, a pen marker, and a trough, after Settlements. You catch one animal and keep it. Dairy lives here once diet exists. Aurochs are the herd animal.

Earth Oven -- a pit that batch-roasts, better than stick-roasting. The pit is the station.

Feasts -- a ritual, gated on Rituals. Feeds recreation and social needs once those exist.

Pit Kiln -- fires clay. 20 leaves, 4 sticks, 4 logs, plus the clay being fired. Tokens and tablets dry here. Only fired pieces count as research.

Pottery -- clay pots that replace coconut shells as cook vessels, with more simmer room. Comes after Agriculture.

Throwing -- atlatl and darts. The Paleolithic ranged option: slower than a bow, worse against armor, fat ammunition.

Dogs -- tame wolves (add wolves). They follow the party, chase game, and can alarm the camp. Taming costs food and time.

Burin -- chisel flake for carving bone and wood. Used for idols, flutes, and tallies. Trees stay on the hand-axe.

Settlements -- unlocks one Manage tab for the Schedule and Assign notes (hour-by-hour schedule, default weapons, default apparel). Handing someone a spear and setting Hunt vs Gather stay available before this.

Ornament -- beads and ochre. Recreation later.

Music -- bone flute, carved with a burin. Recreation later.

Rituals -- a ritual job. Feasts and funerals hang off it. No separate ritual building.

Idolatry -- a carved figurine. If Clay Forming is also known, deity figurines.

Afterlife -- the funeral ritual.

Ancestors -- graves stay as sites. Ongoing rites after the body is buried.

Shrine -- a monument. Needs a hut. Comes before farming.

Mathematics -- clay tokens. One slab makes four. Each token is 1 research point, same as a painting. Forming takes a few hours, drying takes about a day in the pit kiln. Unstackable, only count inside hearth range. If the basket is destroyed, those points are gone.

Proto-Writing -- a clay tablet worth about 10 points, plus the research bench that makes tablets faster than standing in the circle. Keep the tablet or the tech drops. Paintings stay 1, tokens stay 1.
